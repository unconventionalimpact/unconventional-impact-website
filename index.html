Beyond the obvious strategy

What would your business look like if you innovated outside the lines?

Unconventional Impact spotlights ideas, people, products and organizations who go beyond conventional lines to innovate and impact the world. About us

Stories about thinking beyond what’s conventional

As a media house, our goal is to spotlight the people and projects redefining impact from the edges of industries, geographies and ideas: the founder who built a supply chain out of an informal network, the operator who sold the market before the product existed, the entrepreneur turning constraint into an advantage nobody saw coming. These are not stories about doing things the easy way.

Our mission
